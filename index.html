    Measuring

    ERD

    Effective rim diameter: the diameter where the spoke ends sit inside the nipples. Thread a nipple onto each of two old spokes, drop them through opposite holes, screw each nipple on until the spoke end is flush with the bottom of its screwdriver slot, pull them tight and measure across the spoke ends. Rim makers usually list ERD.

    Flange diameter

    Measure centre to centre across two opposite spoke holes. With an odd number of holes per flange, measure edge to edge across adjacent-but-opposite holes and add one hole diameter. On hub motors this is the big one: often 130–220 mm.

    Centre to flange

    Measure the dropout width (locknut to locknut) and each flange's distance to its locknut. Centre to flange = half the dropout width minus that distance. Measure to the middle of the flange, where the spoke bends.

    Checking the result

    Lengths are rounded to the nearest step you choose. If the two sides round to different stock lengths, that's normal for a dished wheel. Check one spoke in the wheel before ordering a full set: it should end flush with the top of the nipple head.
